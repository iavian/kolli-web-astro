Seattle Garden is a **plantation resort** spread over **2.5 acres** of coffee, pepper,
cardamom and spice gardens in Kolli Hills. With serene, contemplative spots along the
**Panchanathi River** — near the 300-foot Agaya Gangai falls — and its own restaurant,
it's a relaxed, green retreat. The resort currently accepts **couples and family
bookings only**.

## Rooms

- **Single Room** — ~120 sq ft, 1 double bed
- **Standard AC Room** — ~140 sq ft, king-size bed
- **Deluxe AC Room** — ~220 sq ft, king-size bed
- **Family AC Room** — ~440 sq ft, 2 king-size beds
- **Family AC Suite** — ~600 sq ft, 3 king-size beds
- **Family AC Cottage** — ~650 sq ft, 3 king-size beds

- Has an **in-house restaurant** — convenient for meals on the hills.
- Amenities include free Wi-Fi, hot & cold water, EV charging (3.3 kW), power backup,
