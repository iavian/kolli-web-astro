NTR Heritage Resort & Spa (formerly **Nallathambi Resort**) is one of the better-known
stays in Kolli Hills, offering cottage-style rooms close to the main viewpoints and the
botanical garden. With an on-site **spa** and restaurant, it's a comfortable base where
you don't have to travel far to relax or eat.

## Good to know

- Book ahead during weekends, school holidays and the festival season.
- Confirm current tariffs and check-in timings by phone before arriving.
- Mobile network can be patchy — note directions in advance.
