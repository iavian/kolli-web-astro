Kukoo Huts is a **traditional hut-style resort** tucked into a peaceful, greenery-
filled pocket of Kolli Hills (reached via a short ~800 m off-road stretch). Built in a
heritage, home-style way, it leans into experiences — bonfire dinners, fire camp,
outdoor dining and more — under the tagline *"More than a stay — it's a soul reset."*

## Rooms

- **Budget Double Room**
- **Double Room with Mountain View**
- **Family Rooms**
- Non-smoking rooms available

## Dining & activities

- **Home-made multi-cuisine restaurant** (veg and non-veg), with food customised to
  your family's preferences, plus a **bar** and coffee served with mountain views.
- **Games:** carrom, chess, rummy, ludo and **archery**.
- **Experiences:** bonfire dining, fire camp, outdoor dinners, night dancing, a
  **boat house**, and guided nature walks and trekking.
