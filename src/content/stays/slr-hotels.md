SLR Hotels — **Silver Line Retreat** — is a comfortable, activity-friendly resort
nestled in the serene Kolli Hills among misty mountains, greenery and waterfalls. It
aims to pair **adventure with relaxation**, blending scenic, modern rooms with
**trekking, waterfall exploration and nature walks** on one hand and a Kerala
Ayurvedic spa, yoga and meditation on the other — *"find your paradise in the heart of
Kolli Hills."* With a **multi-cuisine restaurant** and a wide spread of games and play
areas, it works well for families, holidaymakers and group events alike.

## Rooms

A wide range of options: **Deluxe Double, Deluxe Twin, Deluxe Triple, Family Room,
Suite Room** and a **Dormitory** for groups.

## Facilities & recreation

- **Indoor games** — table tennis, carrom, chess and board games.
- **Outdoor sports** — badminton, throwball, cricket, seven stones and kho-kho.
- **Kids' play area** — swings, slides, climbers, seesaw and more, plus a safe
  **kids' waterfall** water-play area.
- **Outdoor fitness corner** with ski walker, rotators, leg press and other open-air
  gym equipment.
- **Roof garden** (sunrise views and plantations) and a **rock garden** (sunset views).

## Silver Wellness Spa

The resort's **Silver Wellness Spa** is a Kerala Ayurvedic clinic and natural healing
centre — *"a sanctuary of Ayurveda and natural healing."* Treatments include:

- **Kerala-style Ayurvedic whole-body massage** — a traditional ritual followed by a
  soothing head massage and a full-body massage.
- **Kizhi treatment** — a full-body massage using roasted Ayurvedic herbs packed into
  a muslin bag.
- **Personalised Ayurvedic consultations** — complimentary, after a thorough
  examination.

Guests report benefits like relief from tension, improved circulation, joint-pain
relief and detoxification. The resort also offers **yoga and meditation** for a fuller
rejuvenating retreat.

## Events & meetings

SLR markets itself as a venue with a *"luxurious space and breath-taking views of Kolli
Hills"*, hosting:

- Corporate meetings, board meetings and **technical conferences**
- **Destination weddings** and party get-togethers
- Religious events and HR training modules
- Thematic photoshoots and exhibitions

Hall capacities and packages aren't published online — contact the resort for event
enquiries.
