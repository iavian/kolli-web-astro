Madhu Resort is a small, comfortable stay of **8 guest rooms** in Valavanthinadu, Kolli
Hills. A nice touch for the cool hill evenings: rooms come with **fireplaces**. It's a
straightforward, budget-friendly base with the essentials covered.

## Contact & booking

- Bookable via travel platforms (Trip.com, Yatra, Justdial).

## Good to know

- Amenities include **laundry / dry-cleaning** services, complimentary newspapers in
  the lobby, free Wi-Fi and a **24-hour front desk**.
- Simple and practical rather than feature-packed — confirm current facilities and
  rates when booking.
