Kanna Garden by Bodhi Bungalows is a recently renovated **6-room homestay** in a
beautiful garden setting **near the Arapaleeswarar Temple**, about **10 minutes by car
from Agaya Gangai falls**. Each room has a desk, flat-screen TV and private bathroom,
plus a **balcony with an outdoor dining area and garden views** — a relaxed, scenic
base for exploring the central attractions.

## Rooms

Double and Quadruple room categories (family-friendly), each with a private balcony.

## Dining & activities

- À la carte and vegetarian **breakfast with local specialities**, fruit and juice;
  **packed lunches** available for day trips.
- **Walking tours and bike tours**, plus a picnic area and outdoor play area.

## Contact & booking

- Bookable via travel platforms (Booking.com, MakeMyTrip).
- **Airport transfer** available; Salem airport ~95 km, Trichy ~50-minute drive.

## Good to know

- Free Wi-Fi, private check-in/out, a 24-hour front desk and free private parking.
- An **outdoor fireplace** and garden terrace make for pleasant evenings.
