MS Lotus Resorts & Inn is a comfortable **25-room resort** on the Semmedu–
Neduvalampatty road in Kolli Hills, with rooms across Standard, Deluxe, Suite and
Family categories. With an **in-house restaurant**, breakfast included and a spacious
**banquet hall**, it suits families, couples, corporate groups, pilgrims and adventure
seekers alike. Its own tagline: *"Your right choice to have a comfortable and exciting
holiday in Kolli Hills."*

It markets itself for **astro-tourism**, being close to the
[Kolli Hills Dark Sky Park](/attractions/dark-sky-park), and is handy for the Agaya
Gangai waterfalls and Arapaleeswarar Temple.

## Rooms & tariff

- **Single Room** — from ₹1,500 / night
- **Standard (Double)** — from ₹2,750 / night
- **Family Room** — from ₹12,000 / night
- 25 rooms in total across Standard, Deluxe, Suite and Family categories
