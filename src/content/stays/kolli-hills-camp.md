Kolli Hills Camp is an **eco-friendly camp of 10 wooden cottages** set amid trees,
cool breeze and birdsong about **10 km from Kolli town**. It's a camp in the truest
sense — wooden cottages with basic comforts, unpaved pathways and hearty, hygienic
home-style food — but it's well run and surprisingly well-equipped.

## Activities

Campfire, trekking, swimming, birdwatching, photography, and indoor games like chess
and carrom.

## Contact & booking

- Bookable via Tripadvisor and the camp directly; the owner (**Manimaran**) is known
  for staying in close touch with guests.

## Good to know

- Facilities include a **restaurant**, outdoor fireplace, **swimming pool**, **spa &
  massage**, indoor play area, sun deck, a conference room, room service,
  housekeeping, a 24-hour front desk, **doctor on call** and free parking.
- Eco-friendly and clean, with wholesome food — great for nature lovers who don't mind
  rustic touches.
