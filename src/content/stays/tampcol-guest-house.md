The government guest house offers no-frills rooms at modest rates in a central
location. It's a practical choice if you've planned ahead, as availability is
limited and reservations are often required through official tourism offices.

## Good to know

- Booking process can be bureaucratic — start early.
- Amenities are basic; bring your own toiletries.
- Check current availability with the [tourism office](/contacts).
