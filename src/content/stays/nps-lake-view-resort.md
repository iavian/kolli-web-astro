NPS Lake View Resort is a small, peaceful stay of **9 well-furnished rooms** set
**beside a pond amid oak trees** near Semmedu. It's a calm, budget-friendly base where
the quiet of the hills sets the pace, with campfire evenings and a helpful travel desk
to plan your sightseeing.

## Contact & booking

- Bookable via travel platforms (Yatra, Agoda, Tripadvisor, Travelguru).
- Located near the **petrol bunk at Semmedu** — easy to find.

## Good to know

- Amenities include in-room TV, **private dining**, a travel desk and guide service,
  24-hour water supply, room service, laundry and free parking.
- **Campfire and music** in the evenings.
- A good-value option for travellers who want calm over flashy facilities.
