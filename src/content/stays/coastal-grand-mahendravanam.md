Coastal Grand Mahendravanam is a **3-star hotel** in the **Coastal Grand** group, set
near Semmedu in Kolli Hills with pleasant **mountain views**. It occupies the
Mahendravanam property (at 14/26, Solakiraipatti, Pallakuzhi Selur Nadu) **formerly
run as Mahendravanam VHM Resorts**. Guests particularly
praise the **service and staff** and the **great location**, and it's **pet friendly**
— rare on the hills. A standout perk is its **round-the-clock airport shuttle**, a
genuine rarity here. It sits about **10 minutes' drive from Masila Falls**.

## Rooms

Rooms come with a flat-screen TV (cable), a desk, a private bathroom, a closet and
fresh linen and towels. **Extra beds** are available, and some rooms enjoy the
mountain-view aspect.

## Getting around

- **Arappaleeswarar Temple** — about a 17-minute drive
- **Kolli Hills Entrance Arch** — about a 43-minute drive
- Nearest airport: **Salem (~100 km)** — a 24-hour airport shuttle is available
