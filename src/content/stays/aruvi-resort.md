Aruvi Resort is a **family-friendly, eco-conscious budget resort** set at around
**3,000 feet** in Kolli Hills. It keeps things simple but well-run — clean rooms, an
on-site **traditional Tamil restaurant**, a kids' play area and campfire evenings —
living up to its tagline: *"Modern by service, budget by cost."*

## Rooms & tariff

- **Deluxe Room** (2 adults) — ₹3,000 / night
- **A/C Deluxe Room** (2 adults) — ₹3,500 / night
- **Deluxe Room** (3 adults) — ₹3,500 / night
- **Deluxe Room** (4 adults) — ₹4,000 / night
- **Deluxe Room** (5 adults) — ₹4,500 / night

## Good to know

- On-site **restaurant** serving traditional Tamil food.
- **Campfire needs pre-booking**; indoor carrom is open until 10 PM.
- A **sightseeing cab service** can be arranged for trips to the falls and viewpoints.
- Wi-Fi is complimentary but subject to network availability on the hills.
