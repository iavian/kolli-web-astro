Black Pepper Resorts & Cottages (by IHotels) is a **23-room** property on **Boat House
Road, opposite the Botanical Garden** in Semmedu. Rooms come with a wardrobe,
flat-screen TV and private bathroom, and select rooms have **balconies with views**.
Beyond the comfortable basics, it leans into activities and is **pet-friendly**.

## Rooms

Double, Suite and Family room categories across 23 rooms.
