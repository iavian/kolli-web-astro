RJ Grand Resort is one of the more fully-featured stays in Kolli Hills, built for
travellers who want resort comforts in the hills. With an **outdoor pool**, evening
**campfire**, indoor and outdoor activities and a choice of rooms and suites, it's a
comfortable base for families and groups. Its own tagline sums it up: *"Book your stay
in the coolest resort in the hills."*

## Rooms

- **Deluxe Double Room** — 2 guests, ~181 sq ft, king bed (1 extra bed available, ₹1,000)
- **Deluxe Family Room** — 4 guests, ~316 sq ft, queen bed (1 extra bed available)
- **Grand Suite** — 4 guests, ~583 sq ft, king bed (up to 2 extra beds)

## Good to know

- **No in-house restaurant** — beverages and activities are available on site, but plan
  meals accordingly (there are eateries nearby in Semmedu).
- **Pets are not allowed.**
- Amenities include free Wi-Fi, 24-hour hot water and room service, a children's play
  area, table tennis, meeting space, EV charging and complimentary parking.
