Gambhiraa Resorts is a **nature-focused resort** in Kolli Hills built around the idea
of unwinding amid greenery — its tagline says it plainly: *"Feel at home in the heart
of nature."* A choice of distinctive room styles, an on-site restaurant and in-house
games make it a relaxed pick for couples and families.

## Rooms & tariff

- **Hut Rooms** — premium; from ₹6,000 on weekdays (₹7,000 regular)
- **Brick Rooms** — deluxe; from ₹4,500 on weekdays (₹5,000 regular)
- **Container Rooms** — budget and novel; from ₹4,500 on weekdays (₹5,000 regular)

## Good to know

- Has an **on-site restaurant** with a cozy ambiance — convenient for meals.
- **In-house games** for downtime between sightseeing and treks.
- Weekday rates are lower than weekends/holidays — book ahead for peak dates.
