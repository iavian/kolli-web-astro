The **Agaya Gangai Eco-Hut** is a peaceful, eco-friendly forest stay run by the
**Tamil Nadu Forest Department**, set inside the scenic **Agayagangai Eco Park** in
the Ariyur Shola Reserve Forest, close to the famous Agaya Gangai waterfalls. Each hut
is built from **natural materials** to blend with the green surroundings, yet comes
with comfortable basics like **private balconies, forest views and hot water** — a
great choice for eco-tourism lovers, nature enthusiasts and birdwatchers.

With cool mountain air, nature sounds and stunning views of hills, forests and nearby
waterfalls, it's an ideal getaway for **solo travellers, families and couples** after
a quiet, sustainable retreat. It also serves as the **accommodation base for the Kolli
Hills [Dark Sky Park](/attractions/dark-sky-park) stargazing camps**, with transport
arranged by the department.

## Things to do

- Birdwatching at the dedicated **bird-watching spots**.
- Walking the **nature trails** through the eco park.
- Unwinding on your **private balcony** with forest and valley views.
- Evenings around the **bonfire area**; a **children's play area** keeps families busy.

## Good to know

- **No food is included** — plan your meals; room service may be available, but carry
  essentials and snacks to be safe.
- **Book through the official portal:**
  [tnfdsutrula.com](https://tnfdsutrula.com/Web/eco_hut_view?hut_id=4&hut=agayagangai-eco-hut)
  — confirm current **tariff, capacity and check-in details** there.
- Huts are **non-A/C and non-smoking** — the hill air keeps them cool naturally.
- Nights get cold and the network is limited — pack warm layers and a power bank.
