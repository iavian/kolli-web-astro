A cluster of independent cottages set in a leafy plantation, Hilltop Cottages is the
pick for travellers who want calm surroundings and a bit of privacy away from the
busier resorts.

## Good to know

- Meals may be on request only — confirm food arrangements while booking.
- Carry essentials; the nearest shops are a short drive away.
- Evenings are cool — extra blankets are usually provided.
