Brindhavanam Resort is a **2-star garden resort** near the B. Thannimathipatti bus stop
in Valavanthinadu, Kolli Hills. Rooms are comfortable with a desk, flat-screen TV and a
private bathroom with shower, and **some enjoy mountain views**. With a restaurant,
car hire and conference facilities, it caters to both leisure and business travellers.

## Contact & booking

- **Phone:** [+91 90351 88508](tel:+919035188508)
- Bookable via travel platforms (Booking.com, Justdial).
- Salem airport is about **90 km** away.

## Good to know

- Has an on-site **restaurant** and **24-hour room service**.
- Free Wi-Fi throughout and free private parking.
- **Car hire** and **conference** facilities on site — handy for groups.
