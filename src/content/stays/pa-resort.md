P.A. Resort is one of the larger stays in Kolli Hills — a "star hill resort" with
around **40 cottages** set in the scenic Semmedu area, part of the well-known P.A.
Group (founded by Mr. M. Palanisamy). With a wide range of room sizes, it caters to
**families, groups, bachelors and corporate** visitors, and guests often praise its
calm, scenic setting and friendly staff. A quirky highlight on the grounds is its
**huge "dancing elephant"** attraction.

## Rooms

Around 40 cottages, including large rooms suited to mid-sized families and small
groups. Tariffs are not published online — contact the resort for current rates.

- On-site **restaurant** serving homely food.

- A fully-equipped **conference hall** suits corporate groups and events.
