Maasila Residency is a hilltop stay in **Gundurnadu village, near Masila Falls**, with
a flexible mix of rooms and a strong family- and group-friendly focus. A garden and
terrace with **mountain views** make a relaxing spot to unwind, and the on-site
restaurant means you don't have to head out for meals.

## Rooms

- **Couples Room** — private and peaceful
- **Family Room** — spacious comfort for families
- **Dormitory** — budget beds for solo travellers and small groups
- **10-person Dormitory** — for large groups, friends or student batches

## Contact & booking

- **Website:** [msrkollihills.com](https://msrkollihills.com)
- Bookable via travel platforms (Booking.com, MakeMyTrip).

## Good to know

- **On-site restaurant** serving a variety of cuisines.
- **Pet-friendly** with pet zones and nature trails; a safe **kids' play area** for
  families.
- Free high-speed Wi-Fi, full **power backup**, a 24-hour front desk, room service and
  free private parking.
