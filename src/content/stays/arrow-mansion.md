Arrow Mansion is a **clean, central budget lodge** right **opposite the Semmedu bus
stand**, in the heart of Kolli Hills town. It's an economical, no-frills choice that
works well for **big groups and families**, with plenty of parking and easy access to
buses and local eateries.

## Contact & booking

- **Phone:** [+91 94444 04220](tel:+919444404220)
- Bookable via travel platforms (Yatra, Justdial, Tripadvisor).
- **Check-in / check-out:** 2:00 PM / 12:00 PM.

## Good to know

- **No in-house restaurant or room service** — but you're right in town, with eateries
  nearby.
- Ample parking and a very central location are the main draws.
- A practical, wallet-friendly base rather than a full-service resort.
