Vangal Eco Farms is a **budget-friendly eco-farm stay** tucked into a **forest area**
of Kolli Hills, run by the same team as NPS Lake View Resort. Guests highlight the
peaceful, nature-focused setting and reasonable rates, with staff who are quick to help
— a good pick if you want greenery and quiet over resort frills.

## Location

- Set in a **forest pocket** near Karaiyankadu / Semmedu, Kolli Hills.
- **Find it on the map:**
  [Open in Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps?q=11.2735339,78.3272088)

## Contact & booking

- Bookable via travel platforms; also linked with **NPS Lake View Resort** management.

## Good to know

- **Food is by pre-order only** — a standard **buffet** format, with no à-la-carte; let
  them know your meal plans in advance.
- A nature stay in the forest — pack essentials and expect a rustic, peaceful vibe.
