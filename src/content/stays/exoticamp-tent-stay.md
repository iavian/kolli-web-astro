Exoticamp offers a **tent stay on a working pepper estate**, where mornings come with
birdsong and peacock calls and evenings end around a **bonfire**. It pairs the
outdoors with the basics done well — clean restrooms, round-the-clock electricity,
breakfast, Wi-Fi and parking — and a cottage option is available as a backup for
families and solo women travellers.

It sits within easy reach of the main sights: Seeku Parai viewpoint (~15 min), Agaya
Gangai waterfalls (~25 min) and Masila falls (~35 min).

## Good to know

- Book ahead, especially in peak season.
- Nights on the hills get cold — pack warm layers even in summer.
- Confirm exactly what each package includes (meals, activities, bedding) when booking.
