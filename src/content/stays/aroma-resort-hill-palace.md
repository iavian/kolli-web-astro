Aroma Resort Hill Palace is a comfortable **garden resort** near Semmedu, set within
easy reach of Kolli Hills' main attractions. With a terrace, an **on-site restaurant**
and rooms featuring queen-size beds and meal options, it's a convenient, no-fuss base
for sightseeing.

## Contact & booking

- Bookable via travel platforms (Agoda, MakeMyTrip, Justdial).
- **Check-in / check-out:** 2:00 PM / 12:00 PM.

## Good to know

- Has an on-site **restaurant** with meal options, plus a garden and terrace.
- Free Wi-Fi and free private parking.
- Sometimes listed simply as "Hill Palace" — the same property.
