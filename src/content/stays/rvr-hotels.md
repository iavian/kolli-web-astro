RVR Hotels — officially **RVR Rejoice Villa Resorts** — is a family-friendly,
budget-oriented resort in Kolli Hills. Unlike some hill stays, it has its **own
multi-cuisine restaurant**, plus a notable focus on wellness through an on-site
**SARVA Ayurvedic care centre**. It's well placed for trips to the Arapaleeswarar
Temple and Agaya Gangai waterfalls.

## Rooms

- **Deluxe** — spacious room for 2 guests with living space
- **Villa** — for 4 guests, with 1 hall and 2 bedrooms
- **Deluxe Villa** — premium, for 4 guests, 1 hall and 2 attached bathrooms
- **Dormitory** — large room with separate beds for up to 8 guests (great for groups)

## Contact & booking

- **Phone:** [+91 99432 65999](tel:+919943265999), 96596 65999, 75987 65999
- **Website:** [rvrhotels.com](https://www.rvrhotels.com)
- Extra person charge around **₹1,200** (varies by season).
- **Children under 8 stay complimentary.**

## Good to know

- One of the few Kolli stays with an **in-house restaurant** — handy for meals.
- Facilities include 24-hour hot water (geyser/solar), LED TVs, gated parking, an
  on-site ATM, a 24-hour help desk and cab service.
- **Wheelchair accessible** with **doctor-on-call** — a thoughtful option for elders.
- A 50-person **conference hall** makes it suitable for small group events.
