SVS Hill Stone Resort is a compact **6-room** stay on the Solakkadu–Kulivalavu road in
Kolli Hills. Rooms are **family-friendly with mountain views**, each with a TV, sofa,
private bathroom and free toiletries. It's a quiet, budget-friendly option for
travellers who value calm and good views over big-resort facilities.

## Contact & booking

- Bookable via travel platforms (Booking.com, Agoda).
- About 6 km from the main Kolli Hills area.

## Good to know

- Facilities include a **24-hour front desk**, an **outdoor seating area**, full-day
  security, **car hire**, room service, bicycle parking and free private parking.
- Free Wi-Fi and mountain views from the rooms.
