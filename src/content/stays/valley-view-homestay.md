Run by a local family, Valley View Homestay is an affordable, friendly option. The
hosts can point you to lesser-known spots and arrange simple, tasty vegetarian meals
made with produce from the hills.

## Good to know

- Rooms are simple and clean — set expectations accordingly.
- Inform the hosts in advance about meal timings and any food preferences.
- A good base for budget and solo travellers.
