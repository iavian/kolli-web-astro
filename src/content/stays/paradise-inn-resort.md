The Paradise Inn Resort is a laid-back, **garden-set** stay on Semmedu Road in Kolli
Hills. Rooms are comfortable and practical, with a seating area, a flat-screen TV with
satellite channels and a private bathroom — and the leafy garden is a pleasant place to
unwind. It's also **pet-friendly** (dogs welcome), which is uncommon on the hills.

## Rooms

- **Double Bed Room**
- **Deluxe Room**
- **Cottage**
- **Dormitory** (good for groups)

## Contact & booking

- **Website:** [theparadiseinn.in](https://www.theparadiseinn.in) ·
  [theparadiseinnresort.in](http://www.theparadiseinnresort.in/)
- Also bookable via travel platforms (Tripadvisor, Airpaz, Travelmyth).

## Good to know

- **Pet friendly** — dogs are welcome; confirm pet policy when booking.
- Free Wi-Fi and free private parking; garden views from the rooms.
- Note: the property has **no pool, spa or gym**.
