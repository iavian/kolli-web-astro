Camp Nomad by the Lake is a **lakeside camping experience** in the Kolli Hills, set
close to the Boathouse area. It's aimed at travellers who want to stay close to
nature, with the calm of the lake and hills around them.

## Good to know

- Contact the camp ahead to confirm availability, packages and exactly what's
  included (meals, bedding, washrooms, activities).
- Nights on the hills get cold — pack warm layers even in summer.
- Carry a torch/power bank; lighting and mobile network can be limited.
- Pair it with a visit to the nearby [Vasalurpatty Boat House](/attractions/vasalurpatty-boathouse).
