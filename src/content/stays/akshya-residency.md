Akshya Residency is a clean, friendly **6-room guesthouse** near the **Semmedu bus
stand** (behind Indian Bank), praised by guests for well-maintained rooms and helpful
staff. Each room has a flat-screen TV with satellite channels and tea/coffee making
facilities, and **breakfast is complimentary** — good value in a central spot.

## Contact & booking

- **Phone:** [+91 93611 30963](tel:+919361130963)
- Bookable via travel platforms (Trip.com, Tripadvisor).
- About **4 km from Masila Falls**; Salem airport ~65 km.

## Good to know

- **Free breakfast**, Wi-Fi, parking and ironing facilities.
- A comfortable budget base close to the bus stand and town amenities.
