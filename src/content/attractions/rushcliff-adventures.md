RushCliff Adventures bills itself as **"India's first nature-powered, adventure-centric
amusement park"** — a thrill-focused park set in the forests of Kolli Hills, near
[Namma Aruvi Falls](/attractions/namma-aruvi-falls) on the Ettukai Amman Temple road.
It pairs wild landscapes with **globally certified equipment, expert-designed tracks
and trained operators**, under the tagline *"No chill, just thrill."*

## Activities

- **ATV / quad bike** rides on forest trails
- **Zipline** adventures
- **Off-road jeep** experiences
- **Trekking** excursions
- A **kids' play zone** (Mini Adventurers Zone)
- A scenic **café** with panoramic valley views
