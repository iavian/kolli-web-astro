The Bend View Point is a spot along the climb where the road opens up to reveal the
**stack of hairpin bends** snaking down the mountainside below you. It's one of the
most photographed views on the way up, capturing just how dramatic the ascent to
Kolli Hills really is.

## Good to know

- Park only in the marked layby and stay well clear of moving traffic.
- Best views are in the **morning**, before haze or fog builds up.
- Keep children and belongings away from the unfenced edges.
- A perfect place to stretch your legs and let brakes cool on the climb.
