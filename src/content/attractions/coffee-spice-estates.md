The cool, misty slopes of Kolli Hills are perfect for **coffee, pepper, cardamom**
and other spices, and the plantations are as much a part of the landscape as the
forests and falls. Many estates offer **guided walks** where you can see traditional
cultivation passed down over generations — from planting through harvest and
processing — wrapped in the aroma of fresh coffee and spice.

## Why visit

- Understand the agro-food traditions that shape life on the hills.
- A relaxed, immersive experience for all ages.
- A great chance to buy **fresh coffee, pepper and spices** at the source.

## Good to know

- Arrange estate visits in advance through your stay or a local guide.
- Wear closed shoes and carry insect repellent for plantation walks.
- Combine with a stop at the [Botanical Garden](/attractions/botanical-garden) to see
  the medicinal plants the hills are known for.
