The Two Stepped Kutti Water Falls is a pretty **two-tiered cascade** in the
**Devanurnadu** part of Kolli Hills. Less visited than Agaya Gangai or Masila, it makes
a peaceful, refreshing escape into the forest for those who like to seek out quieter
spots — and it's well-liked by those who do, with a **4.1★ rating (39 Google reviews)**.

## Location

- **Address:** 69J5+JC6, Unnamed Road, Devanurnadu, Kolli Hills, Namakkal – 637411
  (around 11.2315, 78.3585).
- **Find it on the map:**
  [Open in Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps?q=11.2315445,78.3585215)

## Good to know

- Best flow is during and just after the monsoon.
- **Caution:** streams here can swell quickly after upstream rain — do not enter the
  water if it looks muddy or fast-flowing.
- Wear good grip footwear; rocks near the falls get slippery.
- Carry your own water and snacks, and pack your litter back out.
