Kolli Hills has been known for centuries for its **medicinal plants**, and this
botanical garden is the easiest place to see them. It holds **over 300 plant
species** — including endemic varieties found nowhere else — laid out across a
**rose garden**, a **herbal park** documenting Siddha medicine traditions, fragrant
spices like cinnamon and pepper, and orchids. There's also a **children's park**,
eco-cottages and a valley viewpoint.

## Why visit

- Gentle, well-maintained paths — good for elders and children.
- Learn about the herbs traditionally harvested by the hill tribes.
- A popular picnic spot, with an annual **flower show** drawing crowds.
- A calm green break between the more strenuous waterfall treks.
