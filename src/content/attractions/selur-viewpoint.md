Less crowded than Seekuparai, **Selur Viewpoint** offers a wide, open vista that is
especially beautiful as the sun sets behind the western ranges. It's a good stop if
you want photographs without the crowds.

## Good to know

- The approach road is narrow in places — drive carefully.
- Carry a light jacket; it gets windy and cool toward evening.
- Combine it with nearby villages to see terraced farming up close.
