Namma Aruvi ("our waterfall") is a pretty cascade tucked into the greenery of the
Kolli Hills. Quieter than Agaya Gangai, it's a good choice for travellers who want a
refreshing nature stop without large crowds. The
[RushCliff Adventures](/attractions/rushcliff-adventures) park is nearby, so the two
pair well for a day out.

## Location

- In the **Ariyur Nadu / Devanurnadu** side of Kolli Hills (around 11.2897, 78.3653).
- **Find it on the map:**
  [Open in Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps?q=11.2896713,78.3652898)

## Good to know

- The flow is at its best during and just after the monsoon.
- **Caution:** like all streams here, levels can rise quickly after upstream rain —
  do not enter the water if it looks muddy or fast-flowing.
- Wear footwear with good grip; rocks near the falls get slippery.
- Carry your litter back out and help keep the falls clean.
