Masi Periasamy Temple is a much-loved shrine among the hill communities of Kolli
Hills, dedicated to **Periasamy**, a guardian folk deity worshipped across many
Tamil villages. The deity is believed to have **resided here for more than 1,000
years**. The temple sits at the **top of [Masila Falls](/attractions/masila-falls)**,
so it pairs naturally with a visit to the waterfall, and is as much a peaceful spot
as a place of devotion.

## Why visit

- A glimpse of the living folk-worship traditions of the Kolli Hills villages.
- Calm surroundings and valley views away from the busier viewpoints.
- Special vibrancy during the annual festival, when the temple comes alive with
  rituals, music and large gatherings of devotees.

## Visitor etiquette

- Remove footwear before entering the temple precincts.
- Dress modestly (shoulders and knees covered).
- Ask before photographing rituals or devotees.
- Expect heavy crowds and limited parking on festival days — plan extra time.
