Solakkadu Market is a local **produce / fruits wholesale market** in Kolli Hills, and a
good slice of everyday hill life. Kolli Hills is famous for its **jackfruit, pineapple,
pepper, coffee and spices**, and this is one of the places where that produce is traded
— colourful, busy and authentic, especially in the early hours.

## Location

- **Address:** 885X+P8R, Semmedu Road, Solakkadu, Karavallicombai R.F., Kolli Hills –
  637415
- **Find it on the map:**
  [Open in Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Solakkadu+Market+Kolli+Hills)

## Good to know

- It's primarily a **wholesale market** that gets going **very early** (around 3:00 AM
  on market days) — go early to catch the action.
- A nice spot to pick up **fresh local fruit** and see where the hills' produce comes
  from.
- Carry cash and be mindful that traders are there to do business — keep out of the way
  of loading and weighing.
