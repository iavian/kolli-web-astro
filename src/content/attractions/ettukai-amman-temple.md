Dedicated to **Ettukai Amman** (the eight-armed goddess), a protector figure in the
local tribal traditions, this hilltop temple — also known as the **Kollipaavai
Temple** — is reached by a woodland trek across rugged slopes. Beyond its spiritual
significance — devotees believe prayers offered here are especially potent — it
offers sweeping panoramic views over the valleys and ranges, making it a favourite
for photographers.

## Location

- Set on a hilltop in the **Devanurnadu / Masila Falls** side of Kolli Hills
  (around 11.2865, 78.3744).
- **Find it on the map:**
  [Open in Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps?q=11.2865139,78.3744135)

## Good to know

- The annual temple festival draws **thousands of devotees**, with traditional music,
  dance and rituals — expect heavy footfall and limited parking on those days.
- Wear good footwear for the trek up; carry water.
- Maintain silence and decorum inside the shrine.
- Combine with nearby viewpoints for a half-day cultural outing.
