Sri Naachiamman Temple is dedicated to **Naachiamman**, a form of the village mother
goddess (Amman) worshipped as a protector of the local community. It holds deep
significance for the people of the Kolli Hills and is a serene place to pause amid
the surrounding greenery.

## Why visit

- Experience the living goddess-worship traditions of the hill villages.
- A calm, spiritual stop set against the backdrop of the hills.
- The annual festival brings colourful rituals, music and large gatherings of
  devotees.

## Visitor etiquette

- Remove footwear before entering the temple precincts.
- Dress modestly (shoulders and knees covered).
- Ask before photographing rituals or devotees.
- Expect heavy crowds and limited parking on festival days — plan extra time.
