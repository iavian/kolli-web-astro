Kolli Campers offers a **camping experience** for visitors who'd rather wake up to
mist and birdsong than four walls. Expect pitched tents, bonfire evenings and clear
hill night skies, usually as part of a package that may include meals and guided
activities. It's one of the **highest-rated** spots of its kind, with a **4.8★ rating
across 300+ Google reviews**.

## Good to know

- Book in advance and confirm exactly what the package includes (meals, bedding,
  washrooms, activities).
- Nights on the hills get cold — pack warm layers even in summer.
- Carry a torch/power bank; lighting and network at campsites can be limited.
- Best enjoyed in the dry season; avoid peak monsoon for camping comfort.
