The Vasalurpatty Boat House is a man-made lake set up for tourists, complete with a
small **natural island of boulders** at its centre and **water year-round**. You can
take out **pedal boats and row boats**, and there's a children's play area and snack
stalls. It's an easy, low-effort outing that breaks up a day of viewpoints and treks,
and a lovely spot for couples and families alike.

## Good to know

- Life jackets are provided — insist on wearing them.
- Weekends and holidays get busy; go earlier for shorter queues.
- A good option for families with small children.
