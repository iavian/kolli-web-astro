Seekuparai (also spelled Seeku Parai) is the postcard viewpoint of Kolli Hills. From
its rocky perch you can see across **three districts — Namakkal, Salem and Trichy** —
with views extending **100+ km** on a clear day. On a misty morning the clouds roll
right up to the railing, and at dawn the sky lights up in orange, pink and gold.

Reaching the best spot involves a short, roughly **30-minute walk** through eucalyptus
groves and shola forest from the parking area, so wear comfortable shoes.

## Tips

- Go **before sunrise** — early visibility beats sunset, and haze builds by mid-morning.
- There are rock seating areas and a small park — a calm spot popular for meditation.
- Small stalls sell tea, snacks and local fruit nearby.
- Hold on to children near the unfenced edges.
