Masila Falls is a **tiered waterfall** tumbling through dense Eastern Ghats forest,
with the **[Masi Periasamy temple](/attractions/masi-periasamy-temple) at the top**.
Quieter than Agaya Gangai, it's a scenic stop — and the easy-to-reach lower stretches,
sometimes called the **Mini Falls**, offer shallow, wadeable pools that make a
favourite family picnic spot.

## Good to know

- Best flow is during and just after the monsoon.
- **Caution:** streams here can swell quickly after upstream rain — do not enter the
  water if it looks muddy or fast.
- Visit the Masi Periasamy temple at the top while you're here.
- Pack out your litter; help keep the falls clean.
