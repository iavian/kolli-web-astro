The Kolli Hills Entrance Arch is the landmark that officially welcomes you to the
hills. Standing at the **base of the ghat road**, it's the traditional spot where
travellers stop for their first photograph before beginning the climb of
**70 hairpin bends**.

## Good to know

- A quick roadside stop — pull over safely and watch for passing traffic.
- Great for a group or family photo to mark the start of your trip.
- From here, fuel up and check your vehicle before the winding ascent begins.
- Combine with a short break, as the climb ahead takes time and concentration.
