Tucked into the hillsides are a cluster of caves — by some counts around **18** —
where **Siddhars** (enlightened sages and practitioners of Siddha medicine) are said
to have meditated for centuries. Names associated with the caves include **Bogar,
Agastya and Korakka Siddha**. The smooth, worn rock faces, natural ventilation and
valley-framing apertures hint at their long history of use.

Reaching them is an adventurous trek through forest and medicinal-plant groves, so
it suits travellers who enjoy a bit of effort and a strong sense of place.

## Good to know

- **Hire a local guide** — the route is not obvious and the terrain is rugged.
- Go early in the morning; carry water, a torch and good grip footwear.
- Tread respectfully — these remain spiritually significant sites.
- Avoid the trek in heavy rain when rocks turn slippery.
