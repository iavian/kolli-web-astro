For an honest, cheap and tasty meal, Amma Mess is hard to beat. Expect simple
**vegetarian thali meals**, idli/dosa in the mornings and whatever's fresh that day.
The kind of place where locals eat.

## Good to know

- Limited menu — they cook what's seasonal and fresh.
- Best to arrive at standard meal times before items run out.
- Cash only.
