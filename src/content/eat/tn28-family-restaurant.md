TN 28 Family Restaurant & Coffee Cafe is one of the most popular eateries on the
hills, located conveniently **opposite the Government Hospital, near the Semmedu bus
stop**. Open from early morning for coffee and breakfast through to late dinner,
diners consistently praise its **biryani and chicken dishes** as tasty and generously
served, with a reputation for good value.
