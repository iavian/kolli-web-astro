Kolli Hills is famous for **jackfruit, pineapple, pepper and hill coffee**, and this
little café puts them front and centre. Grab jackfruit chips, seasonal fruit and a
strong filter coffee on your way to or from the viewpoint.

## Good to know

- Pure vegetarian.
- Great quick-bite stop rather than a full meal.
- Seasonal items (like fresh jackfruit) depend on the harvest.
