A dependable full-service restaurant on the main road, Hill Spice serves South Indian
breakfasts, **banana-leaf meals** at lunch and Chettinad-style curries. A solid choice
if you want a proper sit-down meal.

## Good to know

- Lunch (12–3 PM) is the busiest and freshest time for meals.
- Tell them your spice preference — Chettinad dishes run hot.
- Cash is safest; card/UPI may not always work due to network.
