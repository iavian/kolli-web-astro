summary: Water levels at Agaya Gangai, Masila and other falls can rise suddenly during and after rainfall. Do not enter the water when it is muddy or fast-flowing.
---

With monsoon showers active over the hills, streams and waterfalls can swell within
minutes from rain falling **upstream**, even when it looks clear where you are
standing.

## Please follow these precautions

- **Do not enter the water** if it is muddy, rising or fast-flowing.
- Stay behind any barriers and heed instructions from local staff.
- Keep a close watch on children near all water bodies.
- Avoid the long step-descent to Agaya Gangai if steps are wet and slippery.

If conditions look dangerous, postpone — the falls will still be there tomorrow.
