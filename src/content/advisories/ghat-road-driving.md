The only road up to Kolli Hills winds through **70 numbered hairpin bends**. It is
well-paved but demanding, especially for first-time hill drivers.

## Tips for the climb

- Keep to low gears, both up and down — don't ride the brakes.
- **Honk at blind hairpins** and give way to oncoming vehicles.
- Avoid driving up or down after dark or in thick fog.
- Heavy/long vehicles should attempt the climb only in daylight.
- Check your brakes and fuel before starting the ascent.
